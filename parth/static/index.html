How Parth stays true to the Gita. Parth looks up verses from a knowledge base of the Sanskrit text and Swami Swarupananda's 1909 translation, verified word by word against four original scans. It shows the exact verse beside its words, explains who is speaking and why, and never invents verses. Typed answers are also checked by a second AI before you see them.
